Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

SS7 Point Code Converter doesn't collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What the app stores

The app saves only your settings: the point code format you last used, your custom bit layout, whether the German 4-3-4-3 fields are shown, and your theme choices. These are stored on your device. The point codes you enter aren't saved.

No network access

The app doesn't request internet access, so it can't send anything off your device. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking.

Device backups

If you've turned on Android's backup feature, your app settings may be included in your device backup. Google handles that backup under your Google Account settings, and we can't access it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
